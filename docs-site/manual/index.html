使用手册Manual

面向日常使用的完整功能说明。基础流程见入门教程。Complete day-to-day feature reference. For basics see Getting Started.

1 · 主题操作台1 · Theme Console

操作台是全站的控制中枢：主色/副色拾色器与 HEX 输入、名称与分类/行业元信息。所有修改即时生效并同步到预览、导出与令牌输出。副色区提供"自动适配生成"（按当前主色推导候选，首位候选自动加载）与"第三色自动匹配"（按规则以主色为基准生成第三色）。The console is the control center: primary/secondary pickers and HEX inputs, name and category/industry metadata. Every edit applies instantly and syncs to previews, exports and token output. The secondary area offers auto-fit generation (candidates derived from the current primary; the first one auto-loads) and third-color auto-match (rule-based, anchored on the primary).

2 · 配色关系模式与辅助色2 · Harmony Modes & Auxiliary Colors

主体色（主色系）Body color (the series)

主体色支持单色 / 双色 / 三色三种模式，模式由成员数自动派生（1/2/3 个色），无需手动切换：The body color runs in single / dual / triad modes, derived automatically from the member count (1/2/3):

  • 单色模式：副色取主色系深档变体，按钮/进度/标签等组件照常工作；Single: the secondary becomes a deep primary variant — buttons/progress/tags keep working;
  • 双色模式：主 + 副两色；Dual: primary + secondary;
  • 三色模式：增加第三色一等位（独立拾色器、X 删除、拖拽互换）。Triad: adds a first-class third color (own picker, X to delete, drag to swap).

十种关系规则Ten relation rules

"规则补色"下拉将十种经典配色设计映射为可执行规则：互补、冲突、中性（双色可用）与类比、分裂补色、三次色、原色组合、二次色组合、纯色（三色可用；纯色跨 1–3 色）。不适用于当前操作的规则会置灰。选中规则后可"补副色 / 补第三色"。The "rule fill" dropdown maps ten classic designs to executable rules: complement, clash, neutral (dual) and analogous, split-complement, tertiary, primary-combo, secondary-combo, pure (triad; pure spans 1–3 members). Rules that don't apply are disabled. With a rule selected, fill the secondary / third in one click.

辅助色（主体色之外）Auxiliary colors (outside the body)

独立槽位 0..12，与主体色模式无关：手动添加、按规则生成、与主/副/第三一键互换（↔主/↔副/↔三）、上下移、拖拽排序。辅助色进入全部下游产物（CSS 变量、WordPress 色板、Tailwind 色族、渐变色源、分享链接、主题包）。Independent slots 0..12, unrelated to the body mode: add manually, generate by rule, swap with primary/secondary/third (↔Pri/↔Sec/↔3rd), move up/down, drag to reorder. Auxiliaries flow into every output (CSS vars, WordPress palette, Tailwind families, gradient sources, share links, theme packs).

3 · 配色宝典选色3 · Color Baodian Picker

点击"宝典选色"打开弹层：左侧 24 种情感分类，右侧按 CHM 原版式排布的 10 形态 × 1440 组合色卡。点击任意组合即套用到主体色（自动记录快照，"还原用户色"回退）；情感行内 ⚡ 一键套用该情感的首个补色组合。"Baodian pick" opens a modal: 24 mood categories on the left, combo cards in the original CHM layout on the right — 10 forms × 1,440 combos. Clicking a combo applies it to the body color (a snapshot is recorded; "Restore my colors" rolls back). The ⚡ on each mood applies its first complement combo instantly.

3.5 · 语气预设3.5 · Tone Presets

主题库新增"语气"分类：内置 72 个语气预设（24 情感 × 精选 3 个，覆盖单色/双色/三色模式）。每个预设是成套方案——一次应用即得到模式、关系规则、主体色成员与辅助色的完整组合，并可与宝典弹层组合卡上的"整套"按钮配合使用（任意组合一键成套套用）。The theme library gains a "Tone" category with 72 built-in tone presets (24 moods × 3 curated, covering single/dual/triad modes). Each preset is a full suite — one click applies the mode, relation rule, body-color members and auxiliary slots together; the "Full set" badge on any baodian combo card does the same for arbitrary combos.

语气预设为内置对象（不可删除），携带的模式与关系规则会写入分享链接与导出产物；套用后一切仍可在操作台手工调整。Tone presets are built-in (non-deletable); their mode and relation flow into share links and exports. Everything stays editable in the console afterwards.
锚定模式：宝典弹层头部开启"锚定到当前主色"后，组合与语气预设的应用会以你当前主色为锚——主色保持不变，其余成员按组合内的色相关系重推导（各成员的明度/彩度结构保留；超出显示范围时自动收敛）。开关默认关闭，选择会被记住，并同步作用于组合卡、整套按钮与语气预设库应用。Anchor mode: with "Anchor to current primary" enabled in the baodian header, applying combos or tone presets keeps your current primary and re-derives the other members from the combo's hue relations (each member's lightness/chroma structure is preserved; out-of-gamut colors converge automatically). Off by default, remembered across sessions, and honoured by combo clicks, full-set badges and library applies alike.

3.7 · 多源选色3.7 · Multi-source Picker

“宝典选色”按钮已升级为下拉按钮：可切换 宝典配色（情感×形态组合）与 传统色选色（英文命名色 148 / 中国传统色 785 / 日本传统色 693 / 韩国五方色 11，合计 1637 色，全量收录）四个色源，展示序=英文→中国→日本→韩国；弹层结构复用，点击色卡即套用——传统色为“单色即主色”（副色/辅助按规则生成，可改）。色源后续将持续扩充（国旗/知名企业/自然/社会场景）。The baodian button is now a dropdown switching between Baodian combos and Traditional colors (258 cultural named colors from the dacidian, full collection). The modal shell is shared; clicking a traditional swatch sets it as primary with rule-generated secondaries/auxiliaries. More sources (nature/social scenes) are on the way.

国旗选色按大洲浏览各国国旗官方色组（196 国与地区，覆盖联合国成员国与观察员国，争议地区不收录）——卡片带内置真实旗帜缩略（196 张本地资源，完全离线）与配色设计说明，点击即依次填主/副/第三色；知名企业选色按行业浏览 691 个品牌的官方色板（brandcolors.net 一手数据，带品牌 logo 与色彩说明），套用语义相同。National flags browse 196 countries and regions by continent (UN members and observers; disputed territories excluded) — each card shows the real flag thumbnail and a colour design note (official colours; fills primary/secondary/third in order); Brand colors browse 691 official brand palettes by industry (brandcolors.net).

自然选色按天空天象/海洋水体/大地地貌/植物植被/气象/矿物六类提供场景色组（晨曦/晚霞/极光/深海/丹霞/森林…）；社会场景选色按应急消防/医疗/邮政物流/交通/安全/行业惯例/文化礼仪七类提供惯例用色（消防红/医疗绿/邮车绿/交通信号…）。两者均带场景说明，套用语义同国旗/企业。Nature offers six scene categories (sky, ocean, land, plants, weather, minerals); Social conventions offers seven categories (emergency, medical, postal, transport, safety, industry, cultural). Each palette ships a scene note; apply semantics match flags/brands.

4 · 渐变设置4 · Gradients

8 个渐变角色（carousel/hero 等）均可配置：色系来源（主色/副色/第三色/辅助色 N）、色阶端点、角度、前色占比偏置。色系下拉为带色块预览的自定义组件，改一个参数立即反映到所有预览与导出。Eight gradient roles (carousel/hero/…) are configurable: color source (primary/secondary/third/auxiliary N), shade endpoints, angle, and front-color bias. The source dropdown is a custom combo with swatch previews; every change lands in previews and exports immediately.

5 · 主题市场5 · Theme Market

  • 本地库：内置预设 + 自定义预设，支持搜索、分类/行业筛选、应用、删除；Local library: built-in and custom presets with search, category/industry filters, apply and delete;
  • 导入/导出：主题包 JSON（id 冲突自动重命名合并），也可整库导出备份；Import/export: JSON theme packs (id clashes auto-renamed on merge); the whole library can be exported as a backup;
  • 分享链接：URL hash 承载完整主题（含三色/辅助色契约），打开即还原；Share links: the URL hash carries the whole theme (including third/aux contract fields) and restores on open;
  • 远程源：添加 JSON URL 订阅远程预设（内置 HIA 官方示例源），支持多源管理与全部导入。Remote sources: subscribe to remote presets via JSON URL (the official example source is built in), with multi-source management and bulk import.

6 · 网址取色探索（桌面端专属）6 · URL Color Exploration (desktop only)

输入网址后由桌面端真实渲染页面并多信号加权分析（文本/边框/logo/manifest 等来源），输出推荐主副色、置信度、理由与 12 个备选色。应用范围可勾选：应用主副色 与 应用其它色到辅助色（候选色自动填充辅助槽位）。安全上内置 SSRF 双重防护（私网/保留地址拦截 + 逐请求 CDP 级裁决）。暗色双模式自动复采，失败时明确提示且不影响主结果。Enter a URL and the desktop build renders the page for real, scoring it with weighted multi-signal analysis (text/borders/logo/manifest). You get recommended primaries/secondaries with confidence, reasons, and 12 candidates. Apply scope is checkbox-controlled: apply primary & secondary and apply other colors to auxiliary (candidates fill the aux slots). Security includes dual-layer SSRF protection (private/reserved blocking + per-request CDP adjudication). Dark dual-mode rescans automatically; failures are reported explicitly without affecting the main result.

高级用法：环境变量 HIA_CHROME_WS 可连接远程/多实例 Chrome CDP 端点（逗号分隔按序回退）；HIA_CHROME_PATH 指定本机浏览器路径。Advanced: HIA_CHROME_WS connects to remote/multi-instance Chrome CDP endpoints (comma-separated, tried in order); HIA_CHROME_PATH points at a local browser.

7 · 预览区7 · Preview Area

PC 版企业后台：六页示例 × 左右/上下/混合三种结构 × 明暗双模式；设置页实时显示主题名称与主/副/第三/辅助色卡。小程序示例：首页/列表/详情/我的/表单五页。经典支持预览：WordPress 前后台与 Tailwind 落地页的真实场景注入。Enterprise admin: six pages × three layouts × dark/light; the settings page shows the theme name and primary/secondary/third/auxiliary swatches live. Mini-program: five sample pages. Classic targets: WordPress front/admin and a Tailwind landing page with your tokens injected.

预览滤镜（右上角下拉）：透明度淡化 / 低饱和褪色 / 亮度增减 / 灰度 25–100%，以及 色觉模拟（CVD）三项——红色弱 / 绿色弱 / 蓝黄色弱（Machado 2009 严重度 1.0）。滤镜只作用于预览呈现，导出令牌不受影响；切换会记住（localStorage）。Preview filters (top-right dropdown): opacity fade, desaturate, brightness up/down, grayscale 25–100%, plus three CVD simulations — protanopia / deuteranopia / tritanopia (Machado 2009 severity 1.0). Filters affect the preview only, never exported tokens; the choice is remembered.

8 · 界面语言8 · Interface Language

右上角 EN 按钮切换中/英界面；双语切换对演示内容往返无损。导出产物内容不随界面语言变化（数据确定性）。The EN button in the top-right toggles Chinese/English; demo content round-trips losslessly. Export artifacts never change with the UI language (data determinism).

9 · 对比度矩阵与目标生成9 · Contrast Matrix & Target Synthesis

对比度矩阵（主题 Token 导出下方）：把当前主题的全部角色色（主/副/第三/辅助、白、深墨、两个 on 文字色）两两对比，WCAG 2.x 与 APCA 两种读数一表可见；单元格按阈值分档着色，顶部显示当前阈值下的未达标对数。APCA（Lc）是参考值——WCAG 3.0 尚未定案对比度算法，合规判定仍以 WCAG 2.x 为准；Lc 正值=深字浅底，负值=浅字深底。Contrast matrix (below Theme Token Export): pairwise contrast across all role colours (primary/secondary/third/aux, white, ink, and the two on-colours) in one table, with WCAG 2.x and APCA readouts, threshold-banded cells and a failing-pair count. APCA (Lc) is a reference value — WCAG 3.0 has not settled its algorithm; compliance stays with WCAG 2.x. Positive Lc = dark text on light bg; negative = light on dark.

按目标反推文字色：选定目标（3.0 UI / 4.5 AA / 7.0 AAA）后一键为每个角色色给出可读文字色——白字已达标则原样保留（不牺牲可读上限），未达标则保持白字色相、沿亮度二分到目标比值，并给出实测值；不可达时标注"最接近值"。生成结果仅作预览，写回主题属语义 token 周期。Target synthesis: pick a target (3.0 UI / 4.5 AA / 7.0 AAA) and get a readable text colour per role — white is kept when it already passes (never dimming readable text); otherwise the white hue is bisected along lightness to the target with the measured ratio, marked "nearest achievable" when unreachable. Results are preview-only; persisting overrides belongs to the semantic-token cycle.

10 · 语义状态色与圆角/阴影10 · Semantic Statuses & Radius/Shadow

语义状态色（独立面板，ADR-012）：success / info / warning / danger 四组完整色阶——色相按语义固定（绿/蓝/琥珀/红），明度与彩度特征随主色收敛（暗主题不会配出荧光状态色）。点"从主色派生"生成；拾色器逐组覆盖（覆盖后停随主色）；"跟随主色"勾回即重算；"写入辅助槽位"把四组复制进辅助槽，供只认槽位的旧目标端消费。派生是**显式动作**：旧主题不会被静默注入语义色。Semantic status colours (dedicated panel, ADR-012): four full scales — hues fixed by convention (green/blue/amber/red), lightness and chroma converging from the primary (muted themes never get neon statuses). "Derive from primary" generates them; the picker overrides per group (stopping the follow); re-checking "Follow primary" recomputes; "Copy to aux slots" fills slots for legacy targets. Derivation is an **explicit action** — legacy themes are never silently injected.

圆角与阴影（设计令牌操作台内，ADR-006 契约位落地）：圆角基准滑杆派生 sm/md/lg/xl/pill 五档；阴影强度四档（可混入主色做品牌化阴影）；预览 chips 即实物值，所见即导出（CSS/JSON/Tailwind v3/v4 全部携带）。Radius & shadow (inside the design-token console, ADR-006 slot landed): one base slider derives sm/md/lg/xl/pill; four shadow strengths (optionally primary-tinted); the preview chips show the real values that exports carry (CSS/JSON/Tailwind v3/v4).

Tailwind v4 导出目标：新增 CSS-first 的 app.css（@theme 定义 --color-*/--radius-*/--shadow-*，渐变经 var() 动态引用）；v3 tailwind.config.js 保留为兼容形态并同步携带语义色与圆角阴影。Tailwind v4 target: a new CSS-first app.css (@theme defines --color-*/--radius-*/--shadow-*; gradients referenced via var()); the v3 tailwind.config.js stays as the compatibility form and carries the same additions.

导出降级链（TDON-6.8）：渐变变量携带起始色纯色垫底层；注入模板示例改为"先 background-color、后 background-image"双声明——任何一层失效都不露容器底色。Export degradation chain (TDON-6.8): gradient vars carry a solid start-colour underlay; the inject template pairs background-color before background-image — a failing layer never exposes the bare container.

11 · DTCG 令牌互操作11 · DTCG Token Interop

导出：Token 导出目标新增「DTCG .tokens.json（ZIP）」——W3C Design Tokens **2025.10 稳定版**形状（color/dimension/gradient/shadow/fontFamily），可直接导入 Tokens Studio（Figma）、Style Dictionary、Penpot 等生态工具（已实测 Style Dictionary v5 构建出 CSS）。Export: the token export targets gain "DTCG .tokens.json (ZIP)" — the W3C Design Tokens 2025.10 stable shape (colour/dimension/gradient/shadow/fontFamily), directly importable into Tokens Studio (Figma), Style Dictionary and Penpot (verified by an actual Style Dictionary v5 build).

导入：主题库工具栏「导入 DTCG」——白名单解析 color.primary/secondary/semantic 与 radius；色值兼容三形态（2025.10 对象 / 纯 hex 字符串 / 组内 $ref）；超出白名单的组逐条提示（不静默丢弃）；导入值视为用户值（不随主色刷新）。往返一致：HIA 导出的文件可原样导回。Import: "Import DTCG" in the library toolbar — whitelist parsing of color.primary/secondary/semantic and radius; colour values accept three shapes (2025.10 objects / plain hex strings / in-file $ref); out-of-whitelist groups are reported one by one (never silently dropped); imported values are user-managed (they do not follow the primary). Round-trip safe: files HIA exports import back unchanged.
