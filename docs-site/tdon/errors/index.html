错误与警告码Error & Warning Codes

每条问题含 code、path（如 preview.nodes[2].bgToken）与中英双语 message。单次校验最多报 20 条。Every issue carries a code, a path (e.g. preview.nodes[2].bgToken) and a bilingual message. One pass reports at most 20 issues.

E_ 错误：阻止注册Error: blocks registration W_ 警告：提示但可用Warning: noted but usable
